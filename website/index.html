Questions

Asked by committees before they start.

Do residents have to install an app?

No. Everything works in the phone’s browser today. Android apps for residents and guards are on the way and use the same account.

Does the money go through Saral Society?

No. Residents pay directly into the society’s own UPI ID or bank account. Saral keeps the record, checks the proof with the treasurer, and issues the receipt.

How are owner and tenant rates handled?

Set a base rate and a tenant multiplier once. Each month the flat is charged by who lived there. Owner and tenant see the same passbook for the flat.

Can we bring in our existing records?

Yes. Flats, residents and past dues can be imported from Excel or CSV. Rows are checked first, and anything that needs fixing is listed before import.

What about residents who pay in cash or by cheque?

The treasurer records it at the office and the receipt is issued straight away, numbered in the same sequence as UPI payments.

Can a resident pay part of the dues, or pay in advance?

Yes. Any amount can be paid. Anything above what’s owed is kept as credit and used against the next charges automatically.
